1.1 Tricolor Trails 2.0  
(Independence Week Multi-City Series) 
 
Series Summary 
To celebrate the spirit of Independence Week, Boundless launched Tricolor 
Trails 2.0, a nationwide multi-city initiative conducted across 12 cities in 11 
states. Held in collaboration with Nallamala and Sundarbans, the series aimed 
to bring BS students together through cultural exploration, bonding, and 
patriotic celebration. 
From lakes, wildlife sanctuaries, heritage museums, temples, and malls to 
waterfalls, caves, and iconic city parks  each meetup reflected the unique 
character of its location. Across cities like Patna, Nagpur, Bhubaneswar, 
Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Kolkata, Gorakhpur, Jamshedpur, Indore, Jaipur, 
and Chennai, students engaged in introductions, games, local visits, caf├⌐ 
hangouts, and proudly sang the National Anthem as part of the Tricolor Trails 
movement. 
The series successfully connected students nationwide, creating shared 
memories of friendship, unity, and cultural appreciation.

participation from students across Bihar. 
The day began at Gandhi Maidan, where the group assembled before boarding 
the travel bus. The journey quickly turned lively with icebreakers, songs, 
laughter, and engaging conversations that brought everyone closer even 
before reaching the destination. 
The meetupΓÇÖs key highlight was the trek to Dungeshwari Hills, where

Bodhgaya, visiting the Mahabodhi Temple and several serene monasteries.

                                      1.1.2 Nagpur Meetup 
Event OverviewΓÇï
 
Summary 
Boundless hosted a refreshing one-day Nagpur Meetup at the scenic Zilpi 
Lake, creating a day filled with energy, bonding, and patriotic spirit. Supported 
by the IIT Madras BS Student Activity Fee, the meetup brought BS students 
from Nagpur together for a memorable outing. 
The day began with a lively bus ride where participants enjoyed music, dance, 
and icebreaker introductions, setting a cheerful tone right from the start. The 
group first visited the peaceful Sidhivinayak Mandir for darshan before 
heading to the lakeside. 
At Zilpi Lake, students enjoyed the calm ambience, engaging games, and a 
unifying moment of singing the National Anthem as a part of the Tricolor 
Trails initiative-making the meetup truly special. 
The day concluded with a hearty meal at a nearby restaurant, where the fun 
continued with laughter, games, and meaningful conversations.

                                    1.1.3 Bhubaneswar Meetup 
Event OverviewΓÇï
 
Summary 
Boundless hosted a cheerful and energetic Bhubaneswar Meetup at Esplanade 
Mall, bringing together BS students for an Independence-themed gathering 
filled with warmth and enthusiasm. Supported by the IIT Madras BS Student 
Activity Fee, the event carried forward the patriotic spirit of Tricolor Trails. 
Though heavy rains delayed the meetup by a week, the excitement remained 
undiminished. On 14th August, participants finally came together and began 
the session with warm introductions, followed by a proud and unifying 
moment-singing the National Anthem in celebration of Independence. 
After the anthem, the group headed to Burger King in the food court for 
refreshments. Over snacks, students enjoyed lively conversations, shared 
laughs, and bonded with new friends. The meetup concluded with cheerful 
faces, joyful energy, and wonderful memories for everyone who attended.

friendly energy made the activity an unforgettable part of the day.
